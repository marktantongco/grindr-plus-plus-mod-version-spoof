## How to use these

1. Open one site at a time, capture 2-3 folds at 1440x900.
2. Note only the moves that survive a single-accent, spatial-glass system — no brutalist grids.
3. Change one thing per pass, rebuild, re-run `web/scripts/verify.mjs`, re-capture.

Nothing here is a spec. The gate in `web/scripts/verify.mjs` still decides.
